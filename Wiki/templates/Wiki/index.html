The wiki is very much in development stage. It aims to serve as the primary search engine for our entire publications database, further enabled by the capabilities of Postgres vector-based full-text search queries and community-driven content contributions. Querying analyses various parameters across dozens of stored publications metadata such as key word clusters, positioning, and linguistic patterns to name a few. Some of the additional features in development are a personalised dashboard that serves to show you the most relevant information you are looking for.
