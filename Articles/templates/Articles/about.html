About the Project

Words from the Founder

Academic disciplines to this day are often taught very rigidly, often times, at the expense of holistic clarity and real-life application. This project was born out of this very angst, and aims to disseminate the interrelationships between what appear to be distinct areas of knowledge, yet which share a multitude of similarities.

I am Aditya Dedhia, a recent graduate from the international baccaulerate (IB) diploma programme. In Fall 2020, I formally began studying economics and business management with a sustained emphasis on current world affairs. Broadening the scope, I became particularly fond of microeconomics as well as game theory to better understand how firms operate and the decisions they make considering relevant busines tools and theories such as SWOT and STEEPLE analyses that help determine the utilities of the various stakeholders or, agents.

Investigating different learning methods and research over the years across several countries, I noted how traditional methods of module-based instruction do not provide the same experience as exploring by one's own means. With this project being a content-publishing platform, the goal is to extend the role of in our day to day lives and academia in general, and with practical extension: how world economy and businesses function, especially in today's technological and industrial revolution.
