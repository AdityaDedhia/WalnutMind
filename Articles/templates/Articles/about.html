About the Author

I am Aditya Dedhia, a recent graduate from the international baccaulerate (IB) diploma programme. Over the years having experienced an education in the United States, England, Belgium, and Italy, had academic and sociocultural challenges, yet gave me a new outlook on life. Most importantly, it deepened my passion for philosophy, and with practical extension: how world economy and businesses function, especially in today's technological revolution.

In Fall 2020, I formally began studying economics and business management with a sustained emphasis on current world affairs. Broadening the scope, I became particularly fond of microeconomics as well as game theory to better understand how firms operate and the decisions they make considering relevant busines tools and theories such as SWOT and STEEPLE analyses that help determine the utilities of the various stakeholders or, agents.

Apart from reading economics and business management philosophy, I keep active interests in computer science with a focus on web programming as well as philosophical works which may be extrapolated to various decision-making theories in behavioral economics. A common thread throughout my works is this interrelationship between distinct yet vastly interconnected disciplines.
