Words from the founder

I am Aditya Dedhia, a recent graduate of the International Baccaulerate (IB) Diploma Programme. During my studies across four countries and two continents, I would be rather underwhelmed; academic disciplines to this day are often taught very rigidly, often times, at the expense of holistic clarity and real-life application.

  • Maybe I was missing something.
  • That is rather how things have always been...
  • Could a learning drive be imposed?

Traditional methods of module-based instruction do not provide the same experience as exploring by one's own means.

I struggled to understand this myself, until I found something that clicked; knowledge links. Digging deeper into the lore of the scientific literature, this project was born. There is one underlying aim- to disseminate the interrelationships between what appear to be distinct areas of knowledge, yet which share a multitude of similarities.

“With sufficient importance, what needs to be done will be done.”
Aditya Dedhia
